Direction still follows the actor. Recoil voice gain is 1.035, exactly 90% of its prior 1.15; encoded sound files are unchanged.

Foreground samples never loop. Poison/bleed sounds play when a status first starts, not on damage ticks or active refreshes. Healing sounds play at the first tick of a continuous healing episode, not every subsequent one-second tick. A later episode can play again. Derivation reads the authoritative event prefix, so seeking into an active status does not replay its sound. Other accepted control/malfunction/detach events cue once when initiated. Only damaging strike/slash/bite/electric/retaliation events can add the existing recoil cue; support and status ticks cannot. Audio unlock, mute/volume, foreground voice limits, ambient ducking and late/hidden-frame suppression remain.

Several rival crops contained visible pieces from the adjacent pose in the original sheet, particularly Carrion Duke, Coil Saint and Maw Engine. The offline pipeline now isolates each of the five actual rival figure components from the entire master before cropping, instead of relying on a rectangular gutter that cuts through both figures. Original alpha/detail is retained within a one-pixel-expanded component mask. Normalized torso/eyes/shoulder/pivots are rebased through original sheet coordinates to the cleaned crop. Uniform within-set scale remains. Original masters are untouched; all 30 actual decoded rival poses were checked, including the unchanged Iron Widow set.
